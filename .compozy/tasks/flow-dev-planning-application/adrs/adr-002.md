# ADR-002: Keep uncertainties visible without blocking route approval

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-05

## Context

A PlanningDecision includes uncertainties that may motivate its recommended route. Approving a route chooses the next kind of work; this delivery does not execute that work. The source did not specify whether uncertainties were approval blockers. The user selected option A: retain them for the next stage without blocking approval.

## Decision

Show and retain all reported uncertainties before and after approval. The author may approve any supported route while uncertainties remain. Do not require an acknowledgement checkbox, answers, or another analysis to resolve them. Approval must not mark uncertainties as resolved or imply readiness to implement.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Require explicit acknowledgement

- **Description**: Require a separate confirmation that the author has read the uncertainties.
- **Pros**: Captures a distinct acknowledgement.
- **Cons**: Adds a gate beyond choosing and approving a route.
- **Why rejected**: The user selected nonblocking recorded uncertainties without this extra step.

### Alternative 2: Block approval until clarification and reanalysis

- **Description**: Require answers and a new analysis before approval.
- **Pros**: Reduces unresolved information before proceeding.
- **Cons**: Prevents approving a PRD or Tech Spec route whose purpose may be to resolve those questions and adds a clarification workflow.
- **Why rejected**: The user explicitly selected nonblocking behavior.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Uncertainty does not prevent choosing the next appropriate planning activity.
- Readers can still inspect what remained unresolved at approval.

### Negative

- Approval does not certify that the proposed implementation is fully understood.

### Risks

- Users may confuse approval with resolution. Keep uncertainties visible and describe approval as a route decision.

## Implementation Notes

Empty uncertainties are valid and receive an explicit empty state. Missing required decision content or an unsupported route is invalid output, not a nonblocking uncertainty.
