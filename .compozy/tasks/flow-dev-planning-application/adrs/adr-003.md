# ADR-003: Separate route selection from final human approval

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-05

## Context

The source gives the author both “Alterar rota” and “Aprovar planejamento” and requires preserving the AI recommendation and the chosen route. The user selected option A for changes after approval: the route can change during review and becomes read-only once approved.

## Decision

Start review with the selected route equal to the Dev Control recommendation. During review, the author can save another choice from Direct execution, Tech Spec, or PRD. Saving a choice does not approve it. Preserve the original recommendation, its rationale, and whether the current selection follows it (`AI`) or differs from it (`HUMAN_OVERRIDE`). Returning to the recommendation restores `AI` as the current selection source; this field describes the choice, not the absence of human approval.

An explicit “Aprovar planejamento” approves the exact saved decision and route currently reviewed. Persist the approval identity and time. After approval, the decision and selected route are read-only in this delivery; there is no reopening, route editing, or reanalysis of the approved decision. Repeated approval returns the existing outcome without creating another approval or starting any downstream activity.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Approve immediately when a route is selected

- **Description**: Selecting a different route also finalizes planning.
- **Pros**: Uses one fewer action.
- **Cons**: Conflates choosing an option with approving it and makes accidental approval easier.
- **Why rejected**: The requested interaction distinguishes changing the route from explicit approval.

### Alternative 2: Reopen approved planning and retain revisions

- **Description**: Allow later route changes followed by another approval, preserving history.
- **Pros**: Supports changing circumstances.
- **Cons**: Introduces another lifecycle and questions about which approval governs future work.
- **Why rejected**: The user explicitly selected read-only behavior after approval.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Readers can distinguish the AI recommendation, the human choice, and the final approval.
- Approval has a stable meaning for future workflow consumers.

### Negative

- This delivery cannot revise an approved route when circumstances change.

### Risks

- A stale tab could approve an old selection. Require a fresh review if the saved choice changed, and never silently approve a different route.
- `AI` might be mistaken for automatic approval. Present the human approver separately from route-selection provenance.

## Implementation Notes

Define the concurrency and persistence mechanisms in the TechSpec. Preserve a single authoritative decision for the work item. Retrying a failed analysis is recovery, not a new review or approved-decision revision feature.
